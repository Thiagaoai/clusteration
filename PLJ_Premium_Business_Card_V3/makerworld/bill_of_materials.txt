BILL OF MATERIALS — PLJ Carpentry Premium Business Card V3 (per card)
========================================================================

Filament (measured from the actual STL geometry, PLA density 1.24 g/cm3):
  - Bambu PLA Matte Black .......... ~14.3 g  (base / body - models/plj_card_back.stl)
  - Bambu PLA Silk Gold ............. ~0.6 g  (frame, logo, text, QR, inlay - models/plj_card_front.stl)
  - Total (models/plj_card_complete.stl) .... ~14.85 g (matches the 14-15 g target)

Note: the gold detail volume is intentionally small (thin raised relief only,
0.2-0.8mm tall) - most of the card's mass is the solid black base slab.

Consumables:
  - Build plate: Smooth PEI Plate or BIQU CryoGrip Pro (reusable, not
    consumed per print)
  - Optional: glue stick / adhesion aid on plate if first-layer issues occur

Hardware: none (single-piece print, no inserts, no supports, no screws)

Estimated print time (Bambu Lab A1 Mini, 0.2 mm nozzle profile):
  - Approx. 1h30–2h15 per card, depending on AMS purge/flush overhead
    between the two filaments (Smart Purge ON, Flush Volume 0.55).

Notes:
  - Print one card at a time, centered on the plate, as specified.
  - Dry both filaments below 20% humidity before printing (Silk filaments
    are especially moisture-sensitive and prone to stringing/dull finish
    when wet).
