PLJ Carpentry — Premium 3D Business Card (V3)
================================================

A parametric, 3D-printed business card designed for PLJ Carpentry (Cape Cod,
MA). Bicolor matte-black + silk-gold design with a functional, scannable QR
code, a raised circular-saw-blade logo mark, embossed typography, and a
subtle leather-texture back panel.

FEATURES
--------
- 85 x 54 mm, 2.60 mm base thickness, R4 rounded corners, 0.30 mm 45° edge
  chamfer.
- Front: circular-saw-blade logo (no outer ring), "PLJ CARPENTRY" wordmark,
  "CAPE COD • MA" location line, phone icon + number, vertical divider, and
  a real functional QR code linking to https://pljcarpentry.us.
- Back: full leather-texture surface with a small saw-blade mark and
  "PLJCARPENTRY.US" — flush gold inlay, no protrusion (bed-friendly first
  layer).
- QR code sits in a 0.60 mm recessed pocket with 0.80 mm raised modules
  (net +0.20 mm above the surface) — designed and verified to decode
  correctly (tested against an independent QR reader before publishing).
- Gold frame border (+0.40 mm), 3-tier raised logo (0.20 / 0.40 / 0.80 mm),
  embossed main text (+0.70 mm, ≥0.55 mm minimum stroke width for clean
  0.2 mm nozzle printing).

FILES INCLUDED
---------------
- plj_card_complete.stl — full single-body model (preview / single-color
  printing)
- plj_card_front.stl — raised gold-detail geometry (frame, logo, text, QR
  bumps, back inlay)
- plj_card_back.stl — black base body (chamfered shell, QR pocket, leather
  texture)
- plj_card_complete.scad — fully parametric OpenSCAD source (edit text,
  phone number, QR target URL, dimensions, etc.)

PRINT SETTINGS
--------------
Designed and tuned for the Bambu Lab A1 Mini with a 0.2 mm nozzle (0.08 mm
layers, 6 wall loops, 100% infill, ironing on). A 0.4 mm nozzle alternative
profile is included but requires enlarging the secondary text before slicing
— see the included profiles for full details.

MATERIALS
---------
- Bambu PLA Matte Black (base)
- Bambu PLA Silk Gold (details)

HOW TO PRINT (bicolor, no manual painting needed)
--------------------------------------------------
1. Import plj_card_back.stl and plj_card_front.stl together (they share the
   same coordinate origin — do not move either object).
2. Assign Matte Black to the "back" object and Silk Gold to the "front"
   object.
3. Load the included print profile for your nozzle size.
4. Print back-face-down on a Smooth PEI / CryoGrip Pro plate — no supports
   needed.

CUSTOMIZATION
-------------
Everything (phone number, business name, city/state, QR target URL,
dimensions, relief heights) lives in named variables at the top of
plj_card_complete.scad — regenerate the matrix-driven QR either by editing
the embedded QR_MATRIX (advanced) or by re-running the included Python
generator scripts against your own URL.
